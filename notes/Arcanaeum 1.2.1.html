Arcanaeum Modlist Review (Current - ~150–200 mods)

Arcanaeum (by GuitarNinja) is a lightweight "less is more" Vanilla+ Wabbajack/Nexus modlist designed for players who want meaningful enhancements without massive bloat, overhauls, or performance hits. It focuses on curated bug fixes, quality-of-life, balanced gameplay tweaks, and subtle visual improvements while keeping the core Skyrim experience intact. Perfect for older or mid-range hardware.

1. Core Gameplay Philosophy

Pure lightweight Vanilla+.
- No heavy framework like Requiem, full EnaiRim, or SimonRim overkill.
- Philosophy: "Enhance Skyrim, don't overhaul it." Carefully selected mods for bug fixes, light balance changes, and quality additions that feel natural. It stays true to vanilla quests, progression, and atmosphere while making the game smoother and more enjoyable. Ideal "first modded list" or comfort playthrough.

2. Combat System

Modernized vanilla-style combat.
- Core: Blade & Blunt for more weighty, resource-aware, and tactical fighting with better AI.
- Light improvements to animations, hit reactions, and balance. No MCO, BFCO, or heavy Souls-like mechanics. Combat feels more engaging and dangerous than vanilla but remains familiar and accessible for all playstyles.

3. Visuals & Performance

Subtle, clean visual upgrades.
- Uses Skyland AIO textures and other high-quality but lightweight mods for better landscapes, flora, lighting, and NPCs. No extreme parallax or ENB-heavy setups by default.
- Performance: Excellent — one of the lightest large lists.
- Runs smoothly on older/mid-range hardware (e.g., RTX 2060 / i7-7700 at consistent 60 FPS on medium-high settings).
- Small install size (~37–50 GB). Highly optimized with low memory usage and great stability.

4. Quests & Content

Vanilla-focused with light curated additions.
- Vanilla quests and story are preserved and gently enhanced (e.g., JaySerpa quest expansions).
- No massive new lands or bloat — adds meaningful content, gear, and encounters that feel like they belong in the base game. Strong emphasis on quality over quantity.

5. Cities & World

Faithful vanilla world with polish.
- No heavy city overhauls like JK's Skyrim or Great Cities.
- Settlements and landscapes receive subtle texture, lighting, and detail improvements that make the world feel fresher and more immersive while keeping original layouts and atmosphere intact.

6. Stability & Difficulty

Exceptional stability — frequently praised as very clean and reliable.
- Low mod count means fewer conflicts; excellent for long playthroughs.
- Difficulty: Slightly more challenging than vanilla due to combat and balance tweaks, but fair and approachable. Great for casual to dedicated players.

Verdict

Arcanaeum is best suited for players with mid-to-lower end hardware (or anyone wanting a lightweight experience) who desire a clean Vanilla+ Skyrim that enhances the original game without extremes.
It hits the sweet spot of "Skyrim but better" — solid SimonRim-inspired tweaks, improved combat, subtle visuals, and high stability in a small, performant package. More accessible and lighter than Nordic Souls, Winds of the North, or Keizaal, far less demanding than visual powerhouses or hardcore lists like LoreRim. A top recommendation in 2026 for relaxed, immersive playthroughs or as an excellent entry point into modded Skyrim. Highly praised for its "less is more" approach!
