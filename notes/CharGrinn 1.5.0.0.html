CharGrinn Modlist Review (v1.5 - Current, ~1600–2300+ mods)

CharGrinn (by notsafeadults64) is a dark fantasy, visceral, and gritty Wabbajack modlist inspired by Licentia (Classic & Black). It transforms Skyrim into a creepy, tense, and terrifying experience with strong horror elements, gore, atmospheric visuals, and brutal gameplay. It comes in two profiles: a standard one and an OSTIM (adult) version.

1. Core Gameplay Philosophy

Dark Fantasy Horror / Brutal Survival.
- Custom hybrid with heavy survival, combat, and immersion focus (no strict Requiem, full EnaiRim, or SimonRim).
- Philosophy: Turn Tamriel into a dangerous, foggy, haunted nightmare. Combat is visceral and deadly, magic/spells require immersive learning, and the world feels oppressive. Strong emphasis on atmosphere, tension, and a "mature" dark fantasy vibe with gore and (in OSTIM profile) artistic nudity/violence.

2. Combat System

Fast, brutal, and visceral modern combat.
- Core: ADXP/MCO (Modern Combat Overhaul) for fast-paced, aggressive, and cinematic fighting.
- Enhanced with gore, better hit reactions, improved AI, and dangerous enemy behaviors.
- Combat feels intense and punishing early on — enemies are fast and lethal (e.g., frost spiders, Forsworn). Satisfying and bloody once you build up power. Strong support for melee, archery, and limited magic.

3. Visuals & Performance

Creepy, atmospheric, and immersive visuals.
- RAID Weathers for dynamic fog/cover that affects detection, eerie lighting, foggy forests, haunted ruins, and a dark palette. High-quality textures, parallax, and horror-focused effects.
- Performance: Demanding but optimized with profiles.
- Runs on mid-to-high hardware (RTX 3060/4070+ recommended for smooth 1440p). Install size substantial; benefits from a strong GPU for the atmospheric effects.

4. Quests & Content

Vanilla enhanced with dark twists and curated additions.
- Vanilla quests feel darker and more tense.
- Adds immersive spell learning, new encounters, gore mods, and content that heightens the horror/survival feel. Focus on atmosphere and roleplay rather than massive new lands.

5. Cities & World

Dark, tense, and terrifying world.
- Settlements and landscapes are transformed into creepy, foggy, and oppressive environments.
- Exploration feels dangerous and atmospheric with better visibility mechanics (weather/cover) and haunting details. The world rewards caution and preparation.

6. Stability & Difficulty

Solid stability for its size, with consistent praise for cleanliness.
- Active maintenance and community support.
- Difficulty: Very brutal and punishing — early game is traumatizing (even on lower difficulties). Enemies hit hard, combat is fast and deadly. Designed for players who enjoy high tension and challenge.

Verdict

CharGrinn is best suited for players with mid-to-high end hardware who want a dark, gritty, horror-infused Skyrim with brutal MCO combat, creepy atmosphere, and a visceral dark fantasy experience.
It stands out for its unique tense vibe and bloody, intense gameplay — perfect for fans of Licentia-style lists or those seeking something scarier than standard modlists. More horror-focused and brutal than Nordic Souls or Keizaal, less simulation-heavy than hardcore Requiem lists, and a strong choice for mature audiences who enjoy dark, immersive challenges. Highly atmospheric and replayable in 2026 — check the GitHub and trailers for the full (grim) experience!
