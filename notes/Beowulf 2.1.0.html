Beowulf Modlist Review (Current - German Vanilla+)

Beowulf (by Nexusbla18) is a German-language Vanilla+ Wabbajack modlist designed for high-end PCs. It focuses on creating a lore-friendly, immersive experience that looks and feels like a next-gen remaster while preserving the original spirit of Skyrim. It is the first (and currently only) fully German Wabbajack modlist, making it ideal for German-speaking players.

1. Core Gameplay Philosophy

Lore-friendly Vanilla+ — no heavy framework like Requiem, full EnaiRim, or SimonRim.
- Philosophy: Enhance Skyrim visually and with light gameplay improvements while staying 100% true to vanilla lore and atmosphere. No new lands, massive quests, or immersion-breaking additions. Weapons/armors are added only if they fit the original aesthetic. Streamer-friendly (vanilla music, no adult mods).

2. Combat System

Modernized vanilla combat with tactical depth.
- Core: Valhalla Combat for timed blocking, better hit reactions, and more engaging fights.
- Supports both 1st and 3rd person smoothly. Combat feels weighty and improved without turning into full MCO/Souls-like. Light skill system tweaks (Adamant + Scion + Growl) add flavor while keeping progression familiar.

3. Visuals & Performance

Next-gen visual focus — one of its biggest strengths.
- ENB: Rudy ENB with Obsidian Weathers and Seasons.
- Features: Full Lux / Lux Via / Lux Orbis lighting, HD textures with parallax, High Poly Head NPCs, dense forests (Tree Diversity Project + Nolvus Grass), and beautiful landscapes.
- Performance: Demanding but optimized for high-end hardware (RTX 3070+ recommended for smooth 1440p). Excellent visual fidelity with good frame rates on capable PCs.

4. Quests & Content

Strictly vanilla-focused.
- No new major quests or worldspaces added.
- Vanilla quests and story are preserved and enhanced only through better visuals, pacing, and immersion. Focus remains entirely on the core Skyrim experience.

5. Cities & World

Significantly enhanced vanilla world.
- Uses JK's Skyrim, Cities of the North (COTN), The Great Cities/Towns for detailed, populated settlements.
- Lush, dense forests and picturesque landscapes make the world feel alive and premium while respecting original layouts.

6. Stability & Difficulty

Very stable as a curated Vanilla+ list with strong German community support.
- Difficulty: Slightly more engaging than vanilla due to combat tweaks, but approachable and fair. No hardcore survival or deleveling — perfect for relaxed yet immersive playthroughs.

Verdict

Beowulf is best suited for German-speaking players (or anyone comfortable with a German UI) with high-end hardware who want a beautiful, lore-faithful Vanilla+ Skyrim that looks and feels next-gen.
It excels as a polished remaster experience with excellent visuals, light modern combat improvements, and strong immersion without bloat or extremes. More accessible and visually focused than hardcore lists like LoreRim or Wildlander, lighter on content than Nordic Souls or Living Skyrim, and a standout choice for players seeking the "spirit of Skyrim" in a modern package. Highly recommended in 2026 for German modders and high-end visual enthusiasts!
