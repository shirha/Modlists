Tuxborn Modlist Review (Current - ~1500-1700+ mods)

Tuxborn (by Omni/Ouroboros) is a performance-first, content-rich Wabbajack modlist specifically designed for handheld devices like the Steam Deck, while scaling well to mid-range laptops and desktops. It fills the gap for players who want a substantial, modernized Skyrim experience without needing a high-end gaming PC. It uses a smart hybrid of SimonRim and EnaiRim overhauls.

1. Core Gameplay Philosophy

Hybrid SimonRim + EnaiRim with strong performance and content focus.
- Mix of SimonRim (Adamant, Mysticism, Apothecary, etc.) for balanced progression and EnaiRim elements (Vokrii perks or similar in some profiles) for flavorful magic and builds.
- Philosophy: Deliver a fun, content-packed Skyrim that runs smoothly on lower-powered hardware. Enhanced vanilla+ feel with QoL, game fixes, new content, and optional modern combat. Static leveling and new perk trees add depth without extreme overhauls. Lore-friendly and replayable.

2. Combat System

Flexible with vanilla-style and optional modern action.
- Default: Improved vanilla combat using Blade & Blunt (or Plus Combat) with better animations and AI.
- Optional BFCO (Attack Behavior Framework) profiles: Modern third-person action combat with jumping/swimming attacks, combos, and fluid animations (lighter alternative to full MCO).
- Tactical yet accessible — supports melee, magic, and archery well. Great for both traditional and action-oriented players.

3. Visuals & Performance

Performance-optimized with solid modern visuals.
- Uses Community Shaders (full suite in desktop profiles) for efficient parallax, lighting, grass, and effects. No heavy ENB on Deck profiles to maintain FPS.
- Enhanced textures, NPC overhauls, landscapes, and weather that look great on smaller screens.
- Performance: Excellent — built for Steam Deck (40 FPS locked profiles).
- Multiple profiles: Deck (light), Handheld, Desktop, with/without BFCO.
- Runs smoothly on mid-range hardware (16–32 GB RAM recommended). Install size ~190 GB (downloads ~130 GB). Ideal for handhelds, laptops, and lower-end desktops. Shader cache building causes initial stutters but smooths out.

4. Quests & Content

Strong content focus with excellent integration.
- Includes Legacy of the Dragonborn (LotD v6) and a curated suite of compatible quest mods.
- Many additional/overhauled quests, new lands, weapons, armors, followers, and character customization options.
- Vanilla quests feel enhanced with better pacing and new encounters. Exploration and collection are highly rewarding.

5. Cities & World

Enhanced yet performant world.
- Improved NPC/character visuals, populated settlements, and better details without heavy city overhauls that tank performance.
- Lush but optimized landscapes, flora, and interiors that respect vanilla layout. The world feels alive and immersive while maintaining good FPS on handhelds.

6. Stability & Difficulty

Very stable and well-maintained with active Discord support.
- Thoroughly patched for handhelds and various hardware. Regular updates.
- Difficulty: Balanced and approachable — more challenging than vanilla thanks to improved AI and mechanics, but fair and fun. Adjustable via profiles and MCMs. Great for both casual and dedicated playthroughs.

Verdict

Tuxborn is best suited for players on Steam Deck, handhelds, laptops, or mid-range PCs who want a content-rich, smooth, and modernized Skyrim experience without high hardware demands.
It’s the perfect “accessible big list” — blending SimonRim/EnaiRim depth, optional modern BFCO combat, Legacy of the Dragonborn, and solid visuals while prioritizing performance. More approachable and portable than visual powerhouses like Nolvus or Wunduniik, less punishing than LoreRim, and more content-heavy than lighter Vanilla+ lists. Highly recommended in 2026 for anyone wanting hours of fun on the go or on modest hardware. One of the best options for Steam Deck Skyrim modding!
