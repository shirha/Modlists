Detailed Review: Fallout 4 NG Curated (Nexar's Curated Fallout 4 Modlist – 2025/2026 Edition)

Overview: Fallout 4 NG Curated (commonly referred to as Nexar's Curated NG or Next-Gen Curated) is a modern Wabbajack modlist specifically built for the official Fallout 4 Next-Gen update (no downgrade required). It features a carefully selected set of mods (hundreds rather than 1000+ in some kitchen-sink lists) designed to showcase the game's post-Next-Gen capabilities with improved stability, performance, and visuals while preserving the core Fallout 4 experience. It emphasizes quality over quantity, with strong integration of settlement enhancements and graphical upgrades.

Core Gameplay Philosophy

This list follows a refined "Vanilla Plus with enhancements" philosophy. It does not use a heavy total overhaul framework like Horizon's deep systems rework or Lunar Fallout Overhaul (as in Life In The Ruins or parts of LoreOut). Instead, it layers targeted fixes, optimizations, QoL improvements, and visual/texture upgrades directly on the Next-Gen version of the game. The goal is to make the official updated Fallout 4 feel polished, modern, and feature-rich without radically changing core mechanics, combat feel, or the original story identity. It strikes a balance between preservation and meaningful improvements.

Combat & Survival

Combat stays close to traditional Fallout 4 RPG shooter with polish and minor balance tweaks for better responsiveness and feedback. It is not a "Tarkov-lite" hardcore realism experience (no heavy MAIM or extreme wound systems like Fallout Anomaly), nor a deeply tactical/deadly overhaul like LoreOut or Horizon. Gunplay benefits from Next-Gen engine improvements plus curated animation and damage tweaks, making encounters more enjoyable without turning into a power fantasy or ultra-punishing simulator.

Survival elements are optional and accessible via standard Survival mode, but the list is not built around extreme scarcity or resource grind. It suits players who want engaging combat and exploration without constant tension or permadeath pressure.

Visuals & Performance

This is one of the strongest aspects: the list is explicitly designed to deliver a "Next-Gen" visual experience.

  • Weather and lighting: Enhanced dynamic weather and atmospheric lighting using modern community textures and effects that outperform Bethesda's official HD textures.
  • ENB vs. Community Shaders: Likely relies on optimized lighter-to-medium graphical solutions (Community Shaders friendly or light ENB presets) to take advantage of the Next-Gen update's improvements without excessive overhead. Focus is on high-quality textures, improved models, and cohesive ambiance.
  • Performance impact: Excellent optimization for a Next-Gen list. It is tuned for good performance and stability on modern hardware, with fewer CTD risks thanks to careful curation and built-in fixes. Runs smoother than many downgraded heavy lists on equivalent PCs, making it more accessible.

The Commonwealth looks noticeably refreshed with superior textures, clothing variety, and environmental details while feeling like an enhanced version of the base game.

Content & Factions

The list maintains focus on the vanilla storyline with selective enhancements rather than massive expansions.

  • Includes a variety of new clothing, armor, and item options for character customization and immersion.
  • Some new encounters and QoL content, but avoids overloading with dozens of DLC-sized quest mods (e.g., lighter on Tales from the Commonwealth-style additions compared to Magnum Opus).
  • Emphasis is on making existing content feel better through polish, better NPC interactions, and improved world feel.

Settlements & World

Settlements are significantly enhanced with full Sim Settlements 2 integration, turning building into a more engaging and automated pillar without overwhelming complexity. This allows for dynamic settlement growth and management that fits naturally into gameplay.

World-space changes are moderate and lore-friendly:

  • Improved textures, clutter, and foliage for a more detailed, lived-in Commonwealth.
  • No extreme overhauls like full PRP urban density, Desperados conversions, or heavy ReGrowth forests.
  • The world feels refreshed and visually upgraded while keeping the original layout and atmosphere intact.

Stability & Difficulty

  • Stability: One of its key selling points — described as highly stable and performant for a Next-Gen list. Automated Wabbajack installation, pre-resolved conflicts, and optimization patches keep CTDs low. Players note it runs cleaner than many larger downgraded lists, with active maintenance for bug fixes.
  • Difficulty: Tuned for casual to moderate explorers with adjustable settings. Combat and progression feel balanced and approachable rather than punishing. It is not aimed at hardcore permadeath runners (unlike Horizon or Fallout Anomaly) but can be tweaked for more challenge. Ideal for players who want an enjoyable, feature-rich playthrough without frustration.

Verdict

Fallout 4 NG Curated is best suited for players who want a polished, stable, and visually impressive Next-Gen Fallout 4 experience without needing to downgrade the game or deal with heavy overhauls. It is ideal for:

  • Fans of the official Next-Gen update who want it enhanced with better textures, Sim Settlements 2, clothing variety, and QoL fixes.
  • New or returning players seeking an easy, automated install that feels like "Fallout 4 but better" in 2026.
  • Those who prefer a balanced Vanilla+ approach with strong settlement building over extreme survival, massive content bloat, or radical RPG/systems changes.

Comparison to others:

  • Vs. Magnum Opus: Lighter on new quests/weapons/content; more focused on polish and Next-Gen visuals/stability.
  • Vs. LoreOut: Less "modern RPG overhaul" depth (no heavy Lunar/You Are Exceptional skills focus); more conservative and Next-Gen native.
  • Vs. Horizon: Much less systemic depth or hardcore survival simulation.
  • Vs. Life In The Ruins / Fallout Anomaly: Far less punishing survival focus; more accessible and visually driven.
  • Vs. FUSION: Similar Vanilla+ spirit but built natively for Next-Gen with stronger settlement integration and updated visuals.

Avoid if you specifically want a downgraded classic experience, ultra-hardcore realism (Tarkov/Stalker style), deep mechanical overhauls (Horizon), or the absolute maximum new content. It excels as a clean, enjoyable "best of both worlds" list for the current official game version. Check the official site (nexarplays.co.za) and Wabbajack for the latest updates and installation notes.
