Twisted Skyrim Modlist Review (Current - ~3400–5700+ mods)

Twisted Skyrim is a massive, graphics-first total overhaul Wabbajack modlist by TwistedModding. Developed over nearly 4 years as the author's personal "magnum opus," it delivers a complete modern Skyrim experience with stunning visuals, refreshed gameplay, and significant content additions. It offers two profiles: a fully SFW version (great for streamers) and an optional NSFW profile with OStim support.

1. Core Gameplay Philosophy

Graphics-first modern overhaul with SimonRim + custom systems.
- Built around SimonRim (Adamant, Mysticism, Blade & Blunt, etc.) for balanced progression, combined with BFCO combat and extensive custom tweaks.
- Philosophy: Prioritize visual consistency and fidelity above all, while delivering a fresh, engaging gameplay loop. Focus on immersion, new content, and a cohesive "next-gen Skyrim" feel without breaking lore too heavily.

2. Combat System

Modern BFCO action combat.
- Core: BFCO (Attack Behavior Framework) with True Directional Movement, advanced animations, dodging, and improved AI.
- Combat is fluid, aggressive, intelligent, and satisfying — enemies use potions, spacing, and dodging. Feels like a modern action RPG while remaining balanced and fun for various playstyles.

3. Visuals & Performance

One of the strongest visual showcases — the list's primary focus.
- Hand-crafted suite of high-fidelity textures, complex parallax/PBR materials, advanced lighting, weather, and cohesive overhauls. Excellent visual consistency.
- Performance: Very demanding (large install size ~800+ GB total space needed).
- Best on high-end hardware (RTX 4080+ recommended for smooth 1440p/4K). Optimized but resource-heavy — users report 40+ FPS outdoors at 4K on top-tier setups with tweaks.

4. Quests & Content

Significant expansion with new worlds and adventures.
- New & expanded locations, quests, achievements, creatures, and gear.
- Vanilla quests are refreshed and integrated with new content. Strong emphasis on exploration and discovery alongside Legacy of the Dragonborn-style collection elements in some builds.

5. Cities & World

Completely overhauled and expanded world.
- Major visual and content upgrades to cities, settlements, and landscapes for a denser, more immersive feel.
- New worlds/locations add variety while maintaining strong cohesion. Exploration is highly rewarding in a visually spectacular environment.

6. Stability & Difficulty

Good stability for such a large list, with active maintenance and detailed installation guides.
- Difficulty: Balanced modern experience — challenging but fair and rewarding. More power fantasy/action-oriented than hardcore Requiem lists. Adjustable via profiles and MCMs.

Verdict

Twisted Skyrim is best suited for players with high-end hardware who prioritize breathtaking, consistent visuals alongside modern BFCO combat, expanded content, and a fresh gameplay loop.
It stands out as a true passion project with excellent visual fidelity and cohesion. More graphics-focused and modern than many kitchen-sink lists, visually competitive with Nolvus/Wunduniik, and a strong choice for both SFW and optional NSFW playthroughs. Demanding on storage and GPU, but delivers a premium "complete overhaul" experience in 2026. Highly recommended for visual enthusiasts and action fans — check twistedskyrim.com for the full installation guide and gameplay details!
