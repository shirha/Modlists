Licentia Next Modlist Review (Current - ~1500–2100+ mods)

Licentia Next (by akzar / successor to Cacophony's Licentia Black) is a long-running, well-maintained NSFW power fantasy Wabbajack modlist for Skyrim Anniversary Edition. It is built around Legacy of the Dragonborn as a central pillar, with modern combat, strong visuals, and optional/non-intrusive adult content (OStim framework). It emphasizes build variety, stability, and long-term playability.

1. Core Gameplay Philosophy

Power Fantasy with EnaiRim + LotD focus.
- Heavy use of EnaiRim suite (Ordinator, Apocalypse/Odin, Andromeda, etc.) for flavorful, powerful, and highly customizable builds.
- Philosophy: A fun, content-rich, and replayable experience where you become a legendary Dragonborn. Strong emphasis on collection (via LotD), new adventures, and player freedom. NSFW elements are optional and non-intrusive — the list plays perfectly fine without engaging them.

2. Combat System

Modern physics-based and visceral combat.
- Core: Combat Gameplay Overhaul (CGO) + Precision, with dismemberment and gore.
- Fluid, responsive, and satisfying third-person combat with improved animations and enemy AI.
- Supports a wide variety of playstyles (melee, magic, archery) and feels dynamic without being overly punishing. Great for power fantasy runs.

3. Visuals & Performance

Strong, performance-friendly graphics upgrades.
- Improved textures, lighting, parallax, weather, and NPC overhauls that look modern while staying optimized.
- Performance: Well-optimized for its size.
- Runs on mid-to-high hardware (RTX 3060/4070 recommended for smooth 1440p). Multiple profiles and tweaks available. Install size substantial but manageable with good optimization.

4. Quests & Content

Massive content expansion — a major strength.
- Heavy Legacy of the Dragonborn integration with patches for many quest mods.
- Tons of new quests, followers, gear, creatures, and locations. Vanilla quests are enhanced with better pacing and integration. Excellent for exploration and collection playthroughs.

5. Cities & World

Enhanced and lively world.
- Improved cities, settlements, and landscapes through high-quality overhauls and details.
- The world feels populated and full of opportunities for adventure and roleplay while remaining lore-friendly.

6. Stability & Difficulty

Excellent stability — frequently praised as one of the best-maintained large lists.
- Aggressive bug/crash removal and active Discord support. Great for long-term playthroughs.
- Difficulty: Power fantasy leaning — challenging but rewarding with strong progression. Adjustable and fun across many builds.

Verdict

Licentia Next is best suited for players with mid-to-high end hardware who want a fun, content-packed power fantasy Skyrim with modern combat, Legacy of the Dragonborn focus, excellent stability, and optional adult content.
It is a refined successor to the legendary Licentia Black — polished, replayable, and one of the most popular "ultimate experience" lists. More content-heavy and approachable than hardcore Requiem options like LoreRim, visually strong and stable compared to many kitchen-sink lists. Perfect for players who enjoy collecting, powerful builds, and a vibrant Tamriel. Highly recommended in 2026 for its longevity and community support. (NSFW elements are optional and can be ignored.)
